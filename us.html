US FAQs

Do you cover Pacific and Eastern time zones?

Yes — we structure call windows to overlap with both. Most US clients get a single weekly check-in in their local 09:00–16:00 window. Async work continues 24-hour cycle on the engineering side.

Are you SOC 2 / HIPAA / US-compliance ready?

We build with SOC 2 Type II readiness as the baseline — audit logging, access controls, vendor inventory, encryption at rest and in transit. We've shipped into healthcare-adjacent and finance-adjacent environments. HIPAA-specific scoping happens at kickoff.

Yes — Net-15 or Net-30 terms for established US businesses, W-9 on file, payment via Stripe / ACH / domestic wire. Quoted in USD.

Yes. These are our default integration assumptions for US client work. We have production builds running on Stripe, Salesforce, HubSpot, Intercom, Gong, Slack, Zendesk and Intercom in US deployments.
