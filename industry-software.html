AI for software teams who ship.

SaaS companies have the data, dev velocity and APIs. They just don't have the time to weave AI through it all. We embed production AI into your product, your support and your internal ops — without slowing down your release cycle.

The shape of SaaS AI work in 2026

Three years ago "AI feature" meant a chatbot in the bottom-right corner. Now it means a deeply-integrated agent that reads your customer's data, takes actions inside the product, and is trusted enough that you'd put it on a paid tier. Most engineering teams have the skills to build this — they just don't have the bandwidth, the eval discipline, or the architectural opinions to do it without re-scaffolding the whole thing.

The hard part isn't "can we use Claude". It's "how do we ship this without breaking our release cadence, our SOC 2, or our team's nerves".

Where we plug in

  1. In-product AI features. Copilots, intelligent search, summarisation, smart suggestions inside your existing app. We build them inside your codebase — no third-party widget, no separate dashboard your customers have to learn.
  2. Tier-1 support agent. Claude-powered, RAG'd over your help center + internal docs + product data. Confidence-scored and auto-escalates when unsure. Like what we shipped for Operator.io — 71% auto-resolution, CSAT up.
  3. Internal AI ops. The unglamorous wins. Eng productivity, on-call summarisation, AI-drafted release notes, customer-success briefings before each call.
  4. Onboarding & doc agents. An AI that meets every new customer where they are — proactively explains, books office hours, flags risk-of-churn signals.
  5. Eval + observability. The piece no one talks about but everyone needs. We ship a production-grade eval suite, LLM observability with Langfuse, and a regression-detection workflow you can hand to your QA team.

How we work with software teams

Usually one of three engagements:

  • 30-day sprint — one workflow, end-to-end, fully shipped. Same as our playbook describes.
  • Embed-with-your-team — a senior AI engineer sits in your standups for a fixed quarter and ships features alongside your team. Knowledge transfers as it goes.
  • Audit + handoff — half-day deep-dive on your AI roadmap, written recommendations, an opinionated 90-day plan. You take it from there.
