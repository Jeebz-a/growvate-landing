Industry · Real estate AI for brokerages, agents, proptech & property managers

Real estate, on AI autopilot.

From listing copy to lead qualification to nurture, real estate is full of repetitive work that doesn't need a human. We ship AI that handles the busywork so agents can focus on relationships and closings.

What's broken

Agents spend hours writing listing copy, qualifying tire-kickers on the phone, generating market reports for clients, and chasing buyers between portals. The high-leverage work — relationships, negotiation, advice — gets squeezed by all the work that shouldn't need a person at all.

What we ship

  • AI listing generator (photos + data → copy)
  • Lead-qualifier agent (text/voice)
  • Branded market report AI
  • Nurture sequence personalisation
  • MLS / portal / CRM sync

Typical wins

  • Listing copy time: 45 min → 90 seconds
  • Only qualified buyers reach the agent
  • Market reports auto-generated in minutes
  • Lead response time under 2 minutes, 24/7

The real estate AI gap

Every brokerage in the world has access to the same MLS feed, the same Zillow data, the same portals. The advantage isn't information — it's response speed and personalisation at scale. Both are exactly what AI does best, and almost no brokerage has wired this in yet.

The agent who calls back in 2 minutes wins the lead 8× more often than the one who calls back in 24 hours. That stat has been true for a decade. AI is finally how you actually do it.

The five AI plays we ship most often

  1. AI listing generator. Drop in 8 photos and the property facts. Out comes a polished, on-brand listing — long version for portals, short version for social, bullet points for email. We tune the voice to your brokerage in week one.
  2. Lead-qualifier agent. Every inbound lead — Zillow, your site, FB ads, referral — gets an instant text or call from an AI agent. It asks the right questions, confirms budget and timeline, and either books a viewing or marks the lead as not-yet-ready. Your agents only see qualified leads in their calendar.
  3. Branded market reports. A client emails "what's my house worth?" — within 5 minutes they receive a fully-branded, professionally written market analysis with comps, trend charts and your contact info. It looks like you spent two hours on it.
  4. Nurture sequences. Personalised drip campaigns for cold buyers, expired listings, sphere-of-influence — AI-written, personalised per recipient, scheduled across SMS and email. Stops the day the lead replies.
  5. Data sync. Your MLS, your CRM, your marketing tools, your social schedulers — wired together. A listing goes live in MLS and within 5 minutes it's in your CRM, scheduled for Instagram, queued for the newsletter and emailed to matching saved searches.
