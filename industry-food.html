Why food is brutal — and where AI helps

The food industry has the toughest unit economics of any small business. Labour is expensive. Inventory spoils. Demand is unpredictable. Customers are loud on review platforms. The operators we work with don't need a "platform" — they need 4–5 specific automations that quietly compound over a year into the difference between profitability and closure.

A restaurant that forecasts ingredient demand 4 days ahead saves more from reduced waste than from any menu price change.

The six automations that move the needle

  1. Ordering aggregator. One dashboard across Uber Eats, DoorDash, Deliveroo, Just Eat and your own site. No more 4 tablets. AI watches for issues (rejected orders, missing items) and routes accordingly.
  2. Supply chain forecasting. Per-ingredient demand prediction, with auto-orders to your wholesalers. Trained on your historical sales and adjusted for events, weather, promos. Cuts waste meaningfully.
  3. Menu engineering AI. Sales data + ingredient costs + review sentiment → automatic recommendations for menu items to remove, reprice, or promote. Reviewed and approved by you weekly.
  4. Social & marketing assistant. Weekly posts drafted for Instagram, TikTok, Google Business. Specials, behind-the-scenes, food photos. You approve the lot in 5 minutes Sunday night.
  5. Review monitor + AI reply. Yelp, Google, TripAdvisor — every review gets an AI-drafted reply in your voice. You approve or edit. Negative reviews trigger an alert with context (which staff, which day, which menu item).
  6. Loyalty & retention. AI-personalised comms for repeat customers — birthday offers, last-visit reminders, "you usually order on Thursday" nudges. Subtle, on-brand, and not creepy.
