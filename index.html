(07) Questions

Still wondering? Read this.

How much does a Growvate engagement cost?

Most engagements land between $18k–$60k for a 30-day sprint, depending on scope and integration complexity. You see a fixed quote before we start. No hourly billing, no surprise invoices.

How long does it really take to ship?

30 days from kickoff to a live production system, for the standard sprint. Larger multi-system implementations run as back-to-back sprints — but you always have something shipped by day 30.

We don't have an AI strategy yet — can you start from scratch?

Yes. Most clients start with our 5-day AI audit. You walk away with a ranked opportunity map, ROI estimates, and a 90-day execution plan — even if you don't continue with us.

Will this replace my team?

No. We replace your team's worst hours, not your team. The goal is to give your senior people back the 30–60% of their week spent on manual work, so they can do the work only they can do.

Who owns the code and the models?

You do. 100%. Every line of code, every prompt, every workflow lives in your stack, your cloud, your accounts. We hand off everything with full documentation on day 30.

What if it doesn't work?

Every sprint has measurable success criteria defined on day one. If we miss them at day 30, you don't pay the final milestone. We've never had to invoke this — but it's in writing.
